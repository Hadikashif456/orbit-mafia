# NASA Human Research Program (HRP) Countermeasures Reference

# Sleep

## sleep-light-therapy: Circadian & Blue-Enriched Light Therapy
Keywords: sleep, light, circadian, fatigue, insomnia, disruption, rest
Protocol: Implement 30-minute high-intensity blue-enriched light therapy upon waking to stabilize circadian entrainment; strict dark and quiet opportunity windows suggested for flight surgeon review.
Source: NASA HRP: Fatigue Management and Countermeasures
Last reviewed: 2026-11-14

## sleep-hygiene-schedule: Sleep Hygiene and Opportunity Scheduling
Keywords: sleep, fatigue, schedule, rest, restriction, debt, shiftwork
Protocol: Maintain an 8.5-hour quiet rest opportunity schedule with dimmed cabin ambient lighting; non-critical task deferrals suggested for flight surgeon review.
Source: NASA HRP: Fatigue Management and Behavioral Health
Last reviewed: 2026-11-14

# Radiation

## rad-storm-shelter: Solar Particle Event Habitat Storm Shelter
Keywords: radiation, spe, solar, shelter, dose, spike, particle, storm
Protocol: Remain in designated habitat storm shelter module until active dosimeter measures normal transit baseline (<2.0 mSv/day); rescheduling pending EVA spacewalk activities suggested for flight surgeon review.
Source: NASA HRP: Radiation Health Bio-Protection Guidelines
Last reviewed: 2026-11-14

## rad-eva-defer: Deferral of Extravehicular Activities
Keywords: radiation, eva, spacewalk, exposure, flare, dosimeter
Protocol: Postpone pending extravehicular spacewalks during elevated space radiation conditions; limiting personnel proximity to thinly shielded outer habitat hulls suggested for flight surgeon review.
Source: NASA HRP: Space Radiation Bio-Protection Protocols
Last reviewed: 2026-11-14
